# Martin: brief

Martin is a stick figure who lives in my Mac's menu bar. When it's time to sit up, stretch or walk, he slides out from under the notch for about eight seconds, says one light line, and does the move with me. Built for me first; later a C4C show and tell and a writing candidate.

Decided in the grill on Fri 16 Oct 2026, 02:00 to 03:00 Malmö.

## What he is

- A small icon in the menu bar that opens a card when it's time. Martin slides out from under the notch, stays about 8 seconds, and goes back.
- A clean stick figure: round head, two dot eyes, a simple mouth. Light and easy, with real emotion: happy, thinking, walking, proud.
- Name: Martin.

## What he reminds me about

- Posture check every 10 minutes: a quick glance, shoulders back. Fades on its own.
- Sit up every 25 minutes. Fades on its own.
- Stretch every 50 minutes. Martin does the stretch with me (neck rolls, shoulder rolls and so on) with a countdown, then gives a thumbs up.
- Walk every 90 minutes. After 22:00 the walk becomes "stand up and stretch for one minute".
- Stretch and walk stay until I press Done, Snooze 10 min or Skip. When I snooze, Martin walks away.

## How he knows

- Idle detection: 5 minutes with no input counts as a break and resets the timers. No camera.

## When he stays quiet

- While the mic or camera is in use (C4C classes, calls).
- Do not disturb: one switch that stops every interruption until I turn it back on.
- Off on weekends unless I turn him on.

## How he talks

- Light and fun, more dry humour than commands, and the lines change so he never says the same thing twice in a row. Short, plain words, no emojis.

## The rest

- Quiet tally in the menu: "Today: 9 sit-ups, 3 stretches, 1 walk". No streaks.
- Sound: one soft ding when a stretch or walk is done.
- Settings for the rhythms and for which nudges fade on their own.
- Build: native Swift menu bar app in `builds/martin/`, starts at login.

## Design

- Bold door (`skills/design/bold.md`). First test of bold mode.

- Martin's acting (stretching, walking, thumbs up) is the content of the app. The signature motion in `look.md` is how he arrives.
