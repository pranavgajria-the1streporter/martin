# Martin: look

Picked Fri 16 Oct 2026, from the three directions and two rounds of v2. A's size and paper, C's type and copy. Reference: `design/pages/2026-10-16 martin v2.html`.

- **Object:** Martin, a stick figure in black ink, on a small paper card that slides out from under the notch.
- **Colour:** warm paper #F3EFE6 with ink #111111, secondary text #5A564F. No accent colour.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque 800 for what he says. Inter for everything else, the countdown included.
- **Motion:** the card slides out from under the menu bar on a soft spring. Martin acts on loose springs, each part landing a beat after the part it hangs from, and breathes while he waits.
- **Only here:** Martin does the stretch with you, then reacts: a thumbs up, a jump or both arms up when you finish; sad, a head scratch or a shrug when you skip; he turns and walks out the left edge when you snooze.
- **Not this:** a big poster, a macOS notification banner, anything that covers the menu bar.

## Details

- Card width: the notch plus 30 points on each end (250 on a 14-inch MacBook Pro). Below the menu bar, never over it.
- Every line on one line: headline at 17px, subline at 11.5px.
- Stretch counts down 30 seconds and walk 60, the number rolling each second. Both finish on their own at zero.
- Posture and sit-up nudges fade after 8 seconds. Stretch and walk wait for Done, Snooze 10 min or Skip.
- One sound: a soft two-tone ding on Done.
- After 22:00 the walk becomes a minute on your feet.
